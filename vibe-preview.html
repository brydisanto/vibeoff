✦ Architecture of the Upgrade

  • Glassmorphism & Depth Replaced flat black cards with translucent, blurred backdrops backdrop-filter and subtle 1px borders to create spatial hierarchy against the background.
  • Ambient Environmental Lighting Introduced underlying radial gradients and an SVG noise overlay (grain) to eliminate "dead" flat black space and add texture to the arena.
  • Kinetic Micro-Interactions Hovering now scales the inner portrait, levitates the card on the Y-axis, and activates a subtle gold ambient drop-shadow.
  • Premium Typography Pairing Upgraded from standard fonts to 'Bricolage Grotesque' for ultra-heavy, punchy stats/headers, paired with the highly legible 'Outfit' for UI labels.
